Examples and estimates

All figures are Fermi estimates, accurate to an order of magnitude. The ordering between rows is robust to any reasonable choice of parameters; the absolute values are not. Conventions: 1 ideon = 1 minute of someone else's thought at degree 1 and share 1; 1 Vt = 2.5 × 10⁷ ideons; a person has about 350,000 waking minutes per year.

Eve is zero. Under the naive rule "parents get all the minutes of their descendants" she is the leader. Under the principle of irreplaceability she is an artifact of coalescence: had she died childless, someone else would have been Eve, and the population and its minutes would have stayed the same. Parents are credited for upbringing, not for existence.

The average person: 0.4 Vt. Almost all of it is children (about 0.1 Vt each) and close people. Check: 117 billion people times 10⁷ gives 10¹⁸, exactly humanity's budget of minutes.

The professor: one and a half times the average. Thousands of students, but the degree is low (the textbook already exists) and irreplaceability is low (someone else would have taught the same thing). An average scholar's papers come to tens of thousands of ideons, less than one child. The only way to win is an immortal ideon.

Trump: gigantic minutes, tiny degree. People think about him a lot, but he changes the direction of their thought very little. R₀ after his death is probably below one.

The leader among named accounts is a founder of a religion. Only they have all four multipliers large at once: broad reach, deep degree (a frame, not an opinion), a self-sustaining chain and high irreplaceability. Muhammad is probably ahead of Jesus: five prayers a day cost more in minutes, and his share is higher, because half of Jesus's account goes to Paul, the evangelists and Constantine.

The absolute leader is the Anonymous. Language takes part in every minute of every person at a degree close to one, but by Shapley it is smeared across millions. The question "who leads" has two answers: by the ledger, nobody; by name, a prophet.

2. Three languages and one blood

Ben-Yehuda and Zamenhof were born a year apart, both Jews from the Russian Empire, both made a language. Hebrew has about nine million speakers who think in it every minute of their lives; the accumulated minutes since the 1920s are on the order of 10¹⁴, the degree of the language choice is 0.15, and Ben-Yehuda's share in the revival is 5–10 percent (Zionism was already pulling toward Hebrew, and the schools did most of the work). About 50 kVt today, and the account keeps growing, because children get the language for free. Esperanto: about ten million learners over 140 years and about a hundred thousand active speakers, on the order of 3 × 10¹¹ minutes, degree 0.5 (these minutes are entirely voluntary), share 0.4 (he designed the language personally). About 3 kVt, stable. The twentyfold difference is explained by one word: the state. Hebrew is re-emitted through birth, Esperanto through a decision. The state is the most powerful machine against the second law.

James Harrison donated plasma with rare anti-D antibodies 1,173 times over sixty years. The Australian program issued about 2.4 million doses to mothers; without prophylaxis, Rh disease killed or disabled roughly one in twenty at-risk babies, so the program saved on the order of a hundred thousand lives. Harrison's share among the scientists, two hundred donors and medical staff is 3–6 percent: about six thousand infants at a full Vita each and a full 180 degrees, 5–30 kVt. In magnitude, twenty times less than Muhammad and four times less than Trump. But this is an ideon that passed only through the channel of deeds: no words, no reach, only degrees. A plus sign without a single disputed case, coherence of one. By the product I × S × Q, one of the largest named accounts of the twentieth century.

3. A Wikipedia editor

The formula: yearly page views of your articles × 2 minutes of reading × 0.2 (degree: other sources exist) × 0.5 (another editor could have written it, but you did) × authorship share (your bytes relative to the article's size).

A real calculation via the API for an editor of the Russian Wikipedia, active since 2003, with about 900 surviving created articles and ten million page views a year: a rate of about 22 mVt per year, about 0.2 Vt accumulated, and 0.6 Vt after another twenty years at the same rate. That is like two or three raised children. With the average person's background of 0.4 Vt, parity is reached after roughly forty years of editing.

Separately, the founder's share. The Russian Wikipedia gets about 16 billion page views a year, on the order of 250 Vt per year. If the first hundred contributors split even one percent of that, it comes to another 25 mVt per year each, as much as from the articles directly. The share is debatable: the project would have arisen without them, but later and differently.

Wikipedia is the model case of repaying the debt: anonymous ideons by charter, a long chain, high R₀. And this theory cannot be added to it, because original research is prohibited there.

5. What money buys

A billion dollars buys two or three Vitas if spent on minutes, and tens of thousands if spent on degrees.

Channel, $1 billion Calculation Ideons per $ Vt
Advertising 10¹¹ impressions × 5 s × degree 0.01 ~0.1 ~3
Hiring 10⁴ person-years × 10⁵ min × 0.3 × share 0.1 ~0.03 ~1
Film 300 million viewers × 160 min × 0.05 ~10 ~400 for the crew
Saving lives $5,000 per life × 14 million min × share 0.5 ~1,400 ~50,000

The difference between the rows is explained by one thing: the market trades in minutes, not degrees. A minute of attention has a price, about a cent, and the feed and television sell it. A degree has no market, which is why the philosopher is poor and the feed is rich. Charity is arbitrage: degrees at the price of minutes, four orders of magnitude on top. Effective altruists found it first.

6. Art

The only channel where reach and depth cannot be separated: the form is the content, and Tolstoy cannot be paid separately for the ideas and separately for the craft. This is why art is the strongest type of ideon, with the best ratio of half-life to cost. A three-minute song can carry 10¹¹ minutes: the Beatles come to about four thousand Vitas among the four of them. The Mona Lisa, with ten million visitors a year over a century, gave Leonardo only about two Vitas, because the degree of a visit is close to zero: it is a social act, not a shift.

Art shifts perception rather than opinion, and the theory systematically underestimates it. It also becomes anonymous fastest: form is copied without a name, which is why folklore, memes and jokes, the most immortal ideons, are all authorless. Art for art's sake does not exist in the ledger: unheard music equals a forest nobody thought about. Dickinson was zero in her lifetime and much afterwards, and the ledger does not care when.

7. Why some ideons fade faster

Ideon Characteristic time What sustains or kills it
News days the occasion is gone
Image meme weeks displacement, context
Scientific paper years dissolves into the textbook
Pop song decades cheap, charged, but the niche is replaceable
Novel centuries the niche is irreplaceable
Proverb, joke millennia cheap, frequent occasion, no name
Religion millennia institution, ritual, "pass it on", attachment to life
Language indefinite has become the environment

The right-hand column contains neither the word "truth" nor the word "importance". Ideons fade not because they are false or unimportant, but because they are expensive to copy, rarely come up, and are attached to nothing.
