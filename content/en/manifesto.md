# Manifesto

## 1. The meaning of a life is measurable

The meaning of a person's life is how much they changed reality. And the only reality in which anything can mean something is other people's heads. A river no one has ever seen means nothing. So there is one measure: how much of other people's thought you shifted, and by how far.

## 2. The unit

One minute of someone else's thought, shifted by one degree. We call it an **ideon**.

You made a film that a million people thought about for ten minutes: ten million minutes, divided among the crew. You saved a life: every future minute of the person you saved goes to you, in your share. You retold someone else's idea: it took effect in a new head, and the credit goes to you and to the author both. You raised a child: their minutes go to you to the extent that you shaped them.

## 3. Credit flows along the chain

No ideon comes from nowhere. Behind the book stands the author, behind the author a teacher, behind the teacher whoever taught them to read. Credit is distributed along the whole chain, but not equally and not by proximity: each link gets as much as the result would have been missing without it. The reteller gets little, the author much. The city doctor gets little, the village paramedic everything.

## 4. Magnitude and goodness are different axes

Hitler and Gandhi are both enormous in magnitude and stand at opposite ends of the sign. Ideonology measures magnitude first: did you mean anything at all. The sign is a separate module, and it can be assigned by intention, by result, or not at all. We are not replacing ethics with bookkeeping; we are handing ethics a ruler.

## 5. There is a third axis

Besides "how much" and "which way" there is "how precisely." Coherence is the match between what you meant to shift and what you actually shifted. A light bulb and a laser spend the same energy; the laser cuts steel. Meditation produces no ideons and changes no sign. It turns the bulb into a laser.

## 6. Impact is not conserved. Minutes are

An ideon is a flow, not a stock. It dissipates the moment a person forgets. The one thing that never disappears is the budget of minutes: sixty per hour for each of us, population times sixty for humanity. So attention is a zero-sum game, and an ideon lives only by re-emission. Culture is a machine against forgetting.

## 7. There is a threshold

One **Vita** is all the thought of one person over a lifetime, twenty-five million minutes. You received your Vita on credit: from language, from your parents, from your friends, from people you will never know. **Parity** is the moment you have returned as much into other people's heads. The average person dies at 0.4 Vita. The threshold is reachable, but not by default.

## 8. The largest account is unsigned

Language, fire, counting, the wheel, proverbs, jokes. More than half of all impact in history is credited to the Anonymous. This is not an accounting error but a law: a name dissipates faster than content, and the more immortal an ideon, the more anonymous it is. If you want immortality, be prepared not to be in it.

## 9. What follows

Count degrees, not views. Make things that can be detached from you. Save lives where you are irreplaceable. Return the debt to where you borrowed it: into anonymous ideons. Do not confuse fame with trace. And remember that a theory about counting impact is itself an ideon, and everything said here applies to it too.
