# Examples and estimates

All figures are Fermi estimates, accurate to an order of magnitude. The ordering between rows is robust to any reasonable choice of parameters; the absolute values are not. Conventions: 1 peirce = 1 minute of someone else's thought at degree 1 and share 1; 1 Vt = 2.5 × 10⁷ peirces; a person has about 350,000 waking minutes per year.

## 1. Who leads in absolute terms

| Who | Minutes of attention | Degree | Share | Peirces | Vt |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Typical person (median) | ~10⁷ (children, family, friends) | 0.3–1 | 0.5–1 | ~10⁷ | 0.4 |
| Active Wikipedia editor | + 10⁶–10⁷ page views per year | 0.2 | 0.5 | ~2 × 10⁷ | 0.8 |
| James Harrison, blood donor | 4–9 thousand lives for the whole program × 2.5 × 10⁷ | 1 | 0.0003–0.07 | ~3 × 10⁷–2 × 10¹⁰ | 1–700, [by protocol](protocol.html) |
| Donald Trump | ~10¹³ (2015–2026, worldwide) | 0.1 | 0.5 | ~10¹² | 40 k |
| Eliezer Ben-Yehuda | ~10¹⁴ (Hebrew speakers since the 1920s) | 0.15 | 0.07 | ~10¹² | 50 k, growing |
| Euclid | ~3 × 10¹³ (school geometry for 2,300 years) | 0.3 | 0.3 | ~3 × 10¹² | 100 k |
| Karl Marx | ~10¹⁴ (a third of humanity in the 20th century) | 0.3 | 0.3 | ~10¹³ | 400 k |
| Jesus | ~3 × 10¹⁵ (10 billion Christians × 20 min/day) | 0.5 | 0.2 | ~3 × 10¹⁴ | 10 M |
| Muhammad | ~2 × 10¹⁵ (5 billion Muslims, 5 prayers a day) | 0.5 | 0.4 | ~4 × 10¹⁴ | 20 M |
| The Anonymous | ~10¹⁸ (language, counting, fire, norms) | ~1 | distributed | > 5 × 10¹⁷ | > 20 G |

**The typical person: 0.4 Vt.** Almost all of it is children (about 0.1 Vt each) and close people. That is the median; the arithmetic mean is exactly 1 Vt by the first law, and the tail, the Anonymous and the founders of religions, holds the difference. Check: 117 billion people times 10⁷–2.5 × 10⁷ gives on the order of 10¹⁸, humanity's budget of minutes.

**Trump: gigantic minutes, tiny degree.** People think about him a lot, but he changes the direction of their thought very little. R₀ after his death is probably below one.

**The leader among named accounts is a founder of a religion.** Only they have all four multipliers large at once: broad reach, deep degree (a frame, not an opinion), a self-sustaining chain and high irreplaceability. Muhammad is probably ahead of Jesus: five prayers a day cost more in minutes, and his share is higher, because he was prophet and lawgiver in one person, and the text was fixed within the first generation. But both shares are diluted: half of Jesus's account goes to Paul, the evangelists and Constantine; Muhammad's goes to the rightly guided caliphs, the codifiers of the Quran and the collectors of hadith.

**The absolute leader is the Anonymous.** Language takes part in every minute of every person at a degree close to one, but by Shapley it is smeared across millions. The question "who leads" has two answers: by the ledger, nobody; by name, a prophet.

## 2. Three languages and one blood

**Ben-Yehuda and Zamenhof** were born a year apart, both Jews from the Russian Empire, both made a language. Hebrew has about nine million speakers who think in it every minute of their lives; the accumulated minutes since the 1920s are on the order of 10¹⁴, the degree of the language choice is 0.15, and Ben-Yehuda's share in the revival is 5–10 percent (Zionism was already pulling toward Hebrew, and the schools did most of the work). About 50 kVt today, and the account keeps growing, because children get the language for free. Esperanto: about ten million learners over 140 years and about a hundred thousand active speakers, on the order of 3 × 10¹¹ minutes, degree 0.5 (these minutes are entirely voluntary), share 0.4 (he designed the language personally). About 3 kVt, stable. The twentyfold difference is explained by one word: the state. Hebrew is re-emitted through birth, Esperanto through a decision. The state is the most powerful machine against the second law.

**James Harrison** donated plasma with rare anti-D antibodies 1,173 times over sixty years. The first estimate on this page gave him 5–30 kVt and was wrong: the "2.4 million" in the press is doses and mothers, not babies saved. A recount under the [protocol](protocol.html), with rules written before the calculation, gives the whole Australian anti-D program 4–9 thousand lives saved, and Harrison a share of 0.03 to 7 percent, hence 1–700 Vt. Half of the three-order interval is one question: whether the program would have replaced one donor in a small pool. But this is an ideon that passed only through the channel of deeds: no words, no reach, only degrees. A plus sign without a single disputed case in his channel, coherence of one. By I × S × Q, not a record but a specimen: the only account on this site computed under the rules.

## 3. A Wikipedia editor

The formula: yearly page views of your articles × 2 minutes of reading × 0.2 (degree: other sources exist) × 0.5 (another editor could have written it, but you did) × authorship share (your bytes relative to the article's size).

A real calculation via the API for an editor of the Russian Wikipedia, active since 2003, with about 900 surviving created articles and ten million page views a year: a rate of about 22 mVt per year, about 0.2 Vt accumulated, and 0.6 Vt after another twenty years at the same rate. That is like two or three raised children. With the typical person's background of 0.4 Vt, parity is reached after roughly forty years of editing.

Wikipedia is the model case of repaying the debt: anonymous ideons by charter, a long chain, high R₀. And this theory cannot be added to it, because original research is prohibited there.

## 4. What money buys

A billion dollars buys two or three Vitas if spent on minutes, and tens of thousands if spent on degrees.

| Channel, $1 billion | Calculation | Peirces per $ | Vt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Advertising | 10¹¹ impressions × 5 s × degree 0.01 | ~0.1 | ~3 |
| Hiring | 10⁴ person-years × 10⁵ min × 0.3 × share 0.1 | ~0.03 | ~1 |
| Film | 300 million viewers × 160 min × 0.05 | ~10 | ~400 for the crew |
| Saving lives | $5,000 per life × 14 million min × share 0.5 | ~1,400 | ~50,000 |

The difference between the rows is explained by one thing: the market trades in minutes, not degrees. A minute of attention has a price, about a cent, and the feed and television sell it. A degree has no market, which is why the philosopher is poor and the feed is rich. Charity is arbitrage: degrees at the price of minutes, four orders of magnitude on top. Effective altruists found it first.

## 5. Art

The only channel where reach and depth cannot be separated: the form is the content, and Tolstoy cannot be paid separately for the ideas and separately for the craft. This is why art is the strongest type of ideon, with the best ratio of half-life to cost. A three-minute song can carry 10¹¹ minutes: the Beatles come to about four thousand Vitas among the four of them. The Mona Lisa, with ten million visitors a year over a century, gave Leonardo only about two Vitas, because the degree of a visit is close to zero: it is a social act, not a shift.

Art shifts perception rather than opinion, and the theory systematically underestimates it. It also becomes anonymous fastest: form is copied without a name, which is why folklore, memes and jokes, the most immortal ideons, are all authorless. Art for art's sake does not exist in the ledger: unheard music equals a forest that touched no one. Dickinson was zero in her lifetime and much afterwards, and the ledger does not care when.

## 6. Why some ideons fade faster

| Ideon | Characteristic time | What sustains or kills it |
|---|---|---|
| News | days | the occasion is gone |
| Image meme | weeks | displacement, context |
| Scientific paper | years | dissolves into the textbook |
| Pop song | decades | cheap, charged, but the niche is replaceable |
| Novel | centuries | the niche is irreplaceable |
| Proverb, joke | millennia | cheap, frequent occasion, no name |
| Religion | millennia | institution, ritual, "pass it on", attachment to life |
| Language | indefinite | has become the environment |

The right-hand column contains neither the word "truth" nor the word "importance". Ideons fade not because they are false or unimportant, but because they are expensive to copy, rarely come up, and are attached to nothing.
